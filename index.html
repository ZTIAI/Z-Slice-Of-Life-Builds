Installing

Windows: open ZSliceOfLife-setup.exe and click Install. It puts a Z Slice Of Life icon on your desktop and in the Start menu: click it to play. No administrator password needed. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway (the game isn't signed by a big company). To remove it: Settings, Apps, Z Slice Of Life, Uninstall. Your saves are kept.

Linux: unpack it, then run ZSliceOfLife.sh.

Mac: unzip it, then right-click the app, Open, and Open again the first time.

Updates

Download it once. On Windows and Linux the game checks this page when it starts, and offers to update itself: it downloads only what changed, and your saves are kept. (You can turn this off in Preferences.) On a Mac, download the new version from here.
